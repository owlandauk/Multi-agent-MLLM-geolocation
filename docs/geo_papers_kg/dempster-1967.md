# Dempster 1967

Introduces **upper and lower probabilities** from a multivalued mapping — the original generalization of Bayesian inference that Shafer later systematized into belief functions.

## Relation to this thesis
- The historical root of [[dempster-shafer]] and thus of [[dst-module]]. Establishes the conflict-normalization that the thesis diagnoses as flattening the North-America-vs-Europe contrast at coarse levels.
- Precedes [[shafer-1976]], [[yager-1987]].
