# G3

G3 is a multi-stage geolocation method combining retrieval and generation, reporting on the out-of-distribution [[gws15k]] benchmark in addition to Im2GPS3K/YFCC4K.

## Relation to this thesis
- Reports [[gws15k]] — the OOD benchmark the thesis identifies as the winnable battleground (see [[geoclip-rerank-line]]).
- Member of [[retrieval-geolocation-family]].
