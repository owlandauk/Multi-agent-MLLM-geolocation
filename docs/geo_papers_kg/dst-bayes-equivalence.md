# DST-Bayes Equivalence (2602.18872)

Characterizes conditions under which Dempster-Shafer fusion is equivalent to (or diverges from) Bayesian updating.

## Relation to this thesis
- Grounds the thesis claim that **disabling DST at coarse levels reduces the update to plain multiplicative Bayes** ([[geobayes]]) without loss — the equivalence conditions hold when there is no conflict mass to redistribute.
- Extends [[dempster-shafer]], [[dempster-behavior]].
