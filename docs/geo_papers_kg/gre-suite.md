# GRE Suite

GRE Suite benchmarks **geolocation reasoning** quality (not just endpoint accuracy) across MLLMs, scoring the reasoning chain.

## Relation to this thesis
- Evaluation companion to [[georeasoner]], [[geocot]]; provides a reasoning-quality lens complementing distance-threshold accuracy.
- Benchmark sibling of [[llmgeo]], [[geobench-vlm]].
