# IMAGEO-Bench (From Pixels to Places)

Systematic benchmark for evaluating image-geolocation ability of MLLMs across scales, with attention to accuracy AND geographic bias in predictions.

## Relation to this thesis
- Candidate **second evaluation battleground** beyond [[yfcc4k]] — its bias-aware reporting aligns with the thesis focus on over-prediction ([[pure-mllm-country-bias]]).
- Benchmark family: [[systematic-benchmark-geoloc]], [[geobench-vlm]], [[llmgeo]], [[gws15k]], [[where-on-earth-bench]].
